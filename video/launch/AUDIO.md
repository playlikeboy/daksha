All sound in the Debo videos (`debo-launch.mp4`, `debo-launch-vertical.mp4`, `debo-launch.webm`, `debo-demo.mp4`, `debo-demo.webm`) is made by `audio/make-audio.py` in this folder.

| Sound | Where it comes from | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Music: pad, plucked arpeggio, bass, bell tune, kick, snare, hi-hat | Synthesised by `audio/make-audio.py` from sine waves and noise | Our own work, part of this repo |
| Sound effects: whooshes, pops, taps, chime, riser | Synthesised by `audio/make-audio.py` | Our own work, part of this repo |

No samples, loops or recordings from anyone else are used, and no third-party audio (from Openverse or anywhere else) was downloaded. There is no speech.

If you ever add a third-party sound, use only CC0 or CC BY, and add a row here with its title, author, licence and source URL. Credit CC BY sounds where the video is shown.
